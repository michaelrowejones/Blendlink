Blendlink modifications: Three.js expression construction, explicit source
engine evaluation models, serialized working-space matrices, resource ownership,
native RGB/HSV/HSL conversion, signed 32-bit integer utility operations, native image wrap/clip/nearest and cubic sampling, Wave live distortion/roughness with bounded normalized octave sums, integer Brick noise and mortar calculations, and browser/native differential
checks. Native EEVEE sampler behavior is modeled
separately from the Cycles analytical functions.
