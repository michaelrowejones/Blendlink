Blendlink modifications: Three.js expression construction, explicit source
engine evaluation models, serialized working-space matrices, resource ownership,
native RGB/HSV/HSL conversion, signed 32-bit integer utility operations, native image wrap/clip/nearest and cubic sampling, Wave live distortion/roughness and Noise signed FBM and four bounded fractal recurrences with native constant/shader offset precision, recursive coordinates and finite amplitude bounds, integer Brick noise and mortar calculations, per-vertex octahedral normal storage/interpolation models, engine-specific Object/World normal-map functions, and browser/native differential
checks. Native EEVEE sampler behavior is modeled
separately from the Cycles analytical functions.
