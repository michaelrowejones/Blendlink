These retained diagnostics motivated two independent runtime fixes: explicit public PMREM ownership (`814f43d`) and a node-renderer Color background for uniform F32 radiance (`0636c35`). They provide **zero qualification or node-coverage credit**. Fresh integrated family readers remain authoritative.

The original Final failure left four generated PMREM allocations after source disposal. Public asynchronous preparation now owns its result, releases generator scratch and shares the ready result across staging and live scene installation. The exact original 64×32 Float32 source and public IBL generation algorithm remain. Caller adoption preserves a retained result until its new owner disposes it. Optional Three precompile is omitted because its temporary geometry is not owned by the generator.

That fix closed the original strict texture identity check. A separate stricter diagnostic then found one per-scene `SphereGeometry`, named `Background.mesh`, from detached shader preparation. Pinned Three subscribes its cleanup to an internally generated background node; disposing the source Cube does not dispose that node. An explicit public replacement node alone also fails: on the second installation on the same Scene, Three reuses the old background mesh without registering cleanup on the replacement node. A retained node can additionally mask a later caller background assignment. The CPU-only counterexample retains both observations without GPU calls or private-cache changes.

The node-renderer candidate uses `THREE.Color` copied from the original Float32 background pixel. It needs no background mesh or sampler. Classic rendering keeps the existing Cube path. Actual comparison covered five F32 colors, six renderer mappings (Raw, Standard Linear, ACES, AgX, Neutral and Reinhard), and exposure 0.5/2 on Metal and SwiftShader, both WebGPU and WebGL2. All **61,440 linear Float32 words** and **737,280 onscreen RGBA8 channels** matched. The repeated per-mapping linear results in the raw reports are not counted repeatedly.

There is an intentional public type difference: Three's `backgroundIntensity` affects texture backgrounds, while a Color uses its own RGB. The later-intensity control at 0.5 differs on all four tuples (maximum absolute linear difference 0.25). Authored Blender World Strength remains folded into the original F32 radiance. Caller background replacement, Film Transparent, published site overrides and untouched `backgroundNode` ownership have separate host regressions.

A diagnostic replay of eight actual saved Finals on both Metal APIs retained the original strict pixel, texture, resize and disposal controls. Every one of 48 installation/disposal cycles restored the actual geometry count from 2 to exactly 2. Caller mesh/material/callback identities and saved-original-renderer-dispose ownership checks also passed. The unchanged native/Final packet was reused, so this replay is not a fresh numerical qualification and does not establish new native scene appearance credit.
